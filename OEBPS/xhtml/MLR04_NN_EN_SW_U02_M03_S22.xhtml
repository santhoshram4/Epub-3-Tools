Background Knowledge

Story Within a Story

When a character in a book, play, or movie tells a story to other characters, this is called a story within a story. A story within a story often introduces an important message for the characters and for the reader. The bigger plot, or “outer” story, is sometimes called the frame story, especially when it plays a smaller role than the story within a story.

The Last Cuentista

by Donna Barba Higuera

(PART 3)

Purpose for Reading

Read to learn how stories help Petra and others in a new world.

The year is now 2442. Petra awakes from her long sleep on the spaceship, and her family is gone. Even more frightening, the other passengers don’t remember anything about Earth. An evil group called “the Collective” used special technology to block their memories as they slept. Their brains have been programmed to serve the Collective. Everyone has lost their old identity—except Petra. She remembers everything! At a party for new arrivals, Nyla, the Chancellor of the Collective, speaks. Petra has to pretend she is Zeta-1, a girl with no memories, in order to survive. She could be re-programmed—or worse—if anyone finds out the truth . . .

Describe Characters

Characters develop, or change and grow, over the course of a story. They often learn something from the challenges they face. By the end of a story, a character may have a different attitude or perspective. Noticing how characters develop in a story helps readers understand the story more completely.
