Word

Definition

Example Sentence

tra•di•tion (n.)

a belief or a way of doing something that has been passed down through generations

My favorite family tradition is to share stories about someone on their birthday.

cul•ture (n.)

the pattern of beliefs, values, and actions of a specific group of people

Every culture has its own language, music, and art.

pre•serve (v.)

to keep safe

I take pictures to preserve memories of special moments.

a•dapt (v.)

to change because something around you has changed

It can take time to adapt to a new school.
