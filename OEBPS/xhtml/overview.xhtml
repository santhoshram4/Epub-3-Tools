OVERVIEW

This chapter introduces you to botany: what it is, how it developed, how it relates to our everyday lives, and its potential for the future. The discussion includes an introduction to some common questions about plants and their functions, an examination of the scientific method, and a brief review of botany after the invention of the microscope. It concludes with a survey of the major disciplines within the field of botany.
