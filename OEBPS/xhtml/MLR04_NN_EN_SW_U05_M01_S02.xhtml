Background Knowledge

Dragons appear in art and stories from all over the world. In fairy tales from Europe, dragons are fierce creatures who battle knights. In stories from Asian cultures, dragons are often wise and powerful protectors. The pictures show how dragons have been represented in art from both parts of the world.

The Warnings of Shinpai

by Laurie P. Mendoza

Purpose for Reading

Read to find out how villagers solve a problem with a dragon.

In Japanese legend, dragons are often seen as wise, kind creatures that protect people and nature. Sometimes they act as guardians that help keep everything in balance. In this folktale, a dragon called Shinpai looks after a village and its people.

  1. 1:Long ago on a mountaintop in a distant corner of the world lived a dragon named Shinpai (sheen-PIE). From his high perch, Shinpai could see across the sea in one direction and down to the valley in another. Whenever danger was near, he shouted a warning to the people below
  2. 2:“A wave is about to flood the village!” he would call out. “Quickly, up the mountain!” Or, “Invading warriors are marching this way! Prepare to defend yourselves!”
  3. 3:The people of the village trusted Shinpai. They did as he commanded, rushing to higher ground or readying their weapons.
  4. 4:Centuries passed. The village was safe.
