The Ballad of Mulan: The Legend Lives On

by Theresa Liberatore

Purpose for Reading

Read to learn how an ancient story continues to inspire people all over the world.

  1. 1:Her legendary story began as a song. As her fame spread, she inspired plays, novels, movies, video games, and comic books. Even a crater on Venus is named after her!

    crater: a big hole or bowl-shaped pit

  2. 2:She is Mulan. And her story is about 1,600 years old.
  3. 3:“Ballad of Mulan” is a popular Chinese folk song that was composed sometime around the year 400 BCE. It was later written down as a poem. The ballad tells the story of a teenage girl who becomes a celebrated warrior. But her path to glory has an unusual twist.
  4. 4:Mulan’s aging father might be called to war. But Mulan devises a plan to join the army in his place. In Mulan’s time, women can’t serve as soldiers, so Mulan disguises herself as a man. No one suspects her true identity. She goes to war for twelve years, winning battle after battle. She becomes a respected general, known for her courage and skill.
  5. 5:Was Mulan a real person? No one knows for sure. However, we know that her story touched people in a powerful way. Generations of children sang her ballad and passed on her story. Today, Chinese school children still learn the poem by heart. Why has Mulan remained so popular for so long?

Stop and Discuss

What is the Ballad of Mulan?Underline details in paragraphs 3–5 that help you answer the question.

War Stories

  1. 6:The ballad was written during a long, difficult period of conflict in China. The Chinese people battled each other in civil wars. They also fought off invaders from foreign tribes. With the future of their country at stake, brave warriors were the heroes of the day, and Mulan was no exception. Her courage and skill on the battlefield made her an inspiring and patriotic symbol for China.

    at stake: in trouble

  2. 7:Centuries later, China fought new wars with different rivals. Mulan was once again an inspirational heroine. Her story was written in books and acted out in plays. By the 20th century, movies were telling Mulan’s story in a whole new way. The details sometimes changed as the story was told and retold. But Mulan’s legend lived on.

Stop and Discuss

Why did many Chinese people feel inspired by Mulan?Underline details in paragraph 6 that help you answer the question.

The Teachings of Confucius

  1. 8:Mulan also represents important Chinese traditions. Many of China’s social customs are based on the ancient teachings of Confucius, a philosopher who lived during the 5th century BCE. Confucius spread ideas about moral character and social conduct. One of the most important Confucian values is “filial piety.” This means respect for parents and older family members. By taking her father’s place on the battlefield and risking her own life, Mulan showed deep respect for her father. To Chinese readers, this makes her a role model. The teaching of filial piety is still very important in Chinese culture.

    moral character and social conduct: being a kind, respectful person

China and Beyond

  1. 9:In recent decades, Mulan’s influence has reached beyond China’s borders and traveled across the world. Thanks to animated and live-action movies, global audiences are now familiar with the fearless Chinese warrior and her epic battles. This new Mulan may not represent the same historical traditions that are known and celebrated in China. But she still inspires a modern audience with her power, her independent spirit, and her heroism. And thanks to movie sequels and new storylines, Mulan’s legendary tale will continue to find new audiences.
  2. 10:“The Ballad of Mulan” will always have a special place of honor in China. But, like many great stories, it now has a life of its own.

How Words Work

Morphology: Prefixes pre-, post-, mid-

Many words can be broken down into parts that give clues about the word’s meaning

  • • A base word is a word that can stand on its own.
  • • A prefix is a word part that comes before a base word and changes its meaning.

Prefix

Base Word

mid- (during; in the middle)
