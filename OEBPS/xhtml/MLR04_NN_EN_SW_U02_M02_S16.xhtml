Background Knowledge

The poet Ari Tison is a Bribri (BREE-bree)—a member of the largest indigenous group in Costa Rica, a country in Central America. “Indigenous” means that the Bribri people lived in this place long before any other groups arrived. Today, the Bribri inhabit the Talamanca Mountains near the border of Panama, and some live on islands off the coast.

One of the strongest Bribri traditions is storytelling. Many Bribri tales have been told and retold across generations. In the poem “The Storyteller Gets Her Name,” Tison takes pride in continuing the storytelling tradition of the Bribri.

Explain Theme

A theme is a message about life that is expressed through a story or poem.

  • • To find a theme of a poem, think about what the speaker is describing and how they respond to it.

  •  Notice what the poem looks and sounds like. Poets often emphasize, or draw attention to, important details that support the theme.

    • – Which words, sounds, images, or ideas are repeated?
    • – What do you notice about the line breaks and line lengths?

Reread the poem and find text evidence to answer the questions.
