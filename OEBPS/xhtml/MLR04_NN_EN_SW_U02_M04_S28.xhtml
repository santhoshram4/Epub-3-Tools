Review the Prompt and Writing Type: Literary Analysis Essay

PROMPT

Write a four-paragraph literary analysis in which you describe how the impact of storytelling in The Last Cuentista reveals a theme of the story.

A literary analysis essay is a series of paragraphs that explain an idea or a concept from a work of literature in a clear and concise way. The writer uses evidence and explanations to support their ideas.

When you plan a literary analysis, you can plan each of the three main parts: introduction, body paragraphs, conclusion.

Plan an Introduction

An introduction is the first paragraph of a literary analysis. Its purpose is to grab the reader’s attention and introduce the topic. It includes a focus statement that states the main idea of the essay.

  • • The focus statement responds directly to the writing prompt.
  • • The focus statement explains what the essay will mostly be about.

Plan Body Paragraphs

Body paragraphs are in the middle of an essay. They include evidence from the text to support the focus statement. Each body paragraph includes these four parts:

  •  Topic sentence that tells what the paragraph will be about
  •  Evidence or example from the text that supports the topic sentence
  •  Explanation that connects the text evidence to the topic sentence
  •  Sum-up sentence that ends your paragraph with a closing idea

Plan a Conclusion

The conclusion is the last paragraph of the essay. It sums up the important ideas in the essay and reminds readers of your focus statement.

  • • A strong way to begin a conclusion is to restate the focus statement in a new way.
