Unit 2 Glossary of Terms

absolutely completely or certainly

acclimate to change to fit new surroundings

adapt to change because something around you has changed.

ancestors relatives who lived a long time ago

audience a group that listens or watches

bore to force into or pierce

craft a skill in making something by hand

culture The patterns of beliefs, values, and actions of a specific group of people.

distinguish to tell apart or know the difference

elders older adults in a community, respected for their wisdom and experience

embrace to hug or hold lovingly

folklore traditional beliefs and stories

furrow to make grooves or wrinkles in

generation a group of people born around the same time

heritage history or tradition handed down from ancestors

identical exactly the same

incredibly in a way that is hard to believe; extremely

influence effect on other people

intention a plan or purpose

master very skilled or expert

mood state of mind

opportunity a good chance to do something or have success

philosopher someone who studies life, truth, and knowledge

plunge to dive or jump in suddenly

popular liked by many people

preservation the act of keeping something safe from harm

preserve to keep safe

represent to show or stand for something

reveal to show or make known

revolve to move around something; to focus or center on

rhythm a pattern of regular beats

rival someone who tries to win against another

root the beginning, cause, or source of something

siblings brothers and sisters

slur to speak unclearly; to run words together in a way that is hard to understand

snag to grab or catch something, often damaging it

television electronic device that receives and displays images and sound

time capsules containers with objects from an earlier time or culture, saved for future discovery

tradition A belief or a way of doing something that has been passed down through generations

wobble to tremble, rock, or move shakily

wriggle to squirm, twist, and turn
