Editing Focus

Sentence Fragments

A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought.

  •  A complete sentence has a subject and a predicate. The subject tells whom or what the sentence is about. The predicate tells what the subject does or is.

A sentence fragment is a group of words that does not express a complete thought. A fragment is missing a subject or predicate.
