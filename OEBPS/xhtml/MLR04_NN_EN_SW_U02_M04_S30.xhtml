Examine the Checklist

You can use a checklist that is aligned to the rubric to make sure your writing fully answers the prompt and meets all expectations. A checklist is a good tool for identifying ways to make your writing stronger. You can then revise your writing. When you revise, you make changes to a piece of writing to improve it.

Review the checklist and annotation instructions. You will use the checklist to review and annotate your own essay draft.

Literary Analysis Review Checklist

Category

Checklist

Thinking and Ideas
  •  I include a theme of the story. I will put a ★ next to it.
  •  I include text evidence that supports ideas. I will write an E next to each piece of evidence.

Organization
  •  I include a clear and interesting introduction. I will label the introduction with an I.
  •  I include two well-organized body paragraphs. I will circle them.
  •  I include a conclusion that restates the most important ideas and explains why they matter. I will label it with a C.

Language and Mechanics
  •  I use precise language that explains the topic.
  •  I use correct spelling, capitalization, and punctuation.
  •  I use different sentence types that make the writing smooth and interesting.

Review Peer Writing

In a peer review, partners look at or listen to each other’s writing. They help each other make the writing better.

Follow this process to peer review.

1

Listen as your partner reads aloud the draft of their writing. Mark off each item as you hear it.

  •  There is an introduction and it includes a hook to interest readers.
  •  The introduction includes a clear focus statement that relates to the prompt.
  •  The writer signaled each body paragraph with a clear topic sentence.
  •  The body paragraphs relate to the focus statement.
  •  The conclusion adds an extra idea that was not in the introduction.

2

Jot down your observations.

3

Respond to your partner’s writing.

Start with a compliment. Tell your parter something they did well from the checklist.

Something you did well in your literary analysis was _____ because _____.

Then make a suggestion. Tell your partner one way you think the writing could be improved.

I think that if you _____, your literary analysis could be even stronger.

4

Switch roles. Now it’s your turn to read aloud your draft. Your partner will listen and jot down observations using the same process.

  •  As you listen to your partner’s feedback, make revision notes on your draft.
