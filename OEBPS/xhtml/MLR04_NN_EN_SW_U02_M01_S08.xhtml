How Sentences Work

Sentence Fragments

A complete sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete thought.

A complete sentence has a subject and a predicate.

  • • The subject is the part of the sentence that names the person, place, or thing doing the action.
  • • The predicate is the part of the sentence that includes the main verb and shows the action. It describes what the subject does or is.

A fragment is a group of words that does not express a complete thought. A fragment is missing a subject or predicate.

Analyze an Introduction

An essay is a series of paragraphs about a topic. The first paragraph of an essay is the introduction. Follow these steps to write a strong introduction:

  • • Start with a hook, or interesting idea or question that grabs the reader’s attention.
  • • Then introduce the topic with one or more sentences that connect the hook to the main idea in the writing prompt .
  • • End the introduction with a focus statement. This sentence states the main idea of the essay by clearly answering the prompt.

ANNOTATE A MODEL

Why are friends important? In the article “Cy Thao, Story Painter,” Cy learns the value of friendship when he moves to a new country and feels like an outsider. Cy communicated with his classmates by drawing pictures because they didn’t speak the same language. Through his pictures, his friendships grew. Friendship was valuable to Cy when he was young because his new friends helped him feel less alone in a new country.
