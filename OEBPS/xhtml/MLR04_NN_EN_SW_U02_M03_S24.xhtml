How Sentences Work

Verb Tense

Verbs are action words. They show what something is or does and describe what the subject of the sentence is doing.

  • • Verb tenses are different forms of a verb that show when an action takes place. Simple verb tenses include present (I listen), past (I listened), and future (I will listen).
  • • When you write, use the tense that shows the time of the action. Change the tense if you need to show events happening at different times.
  • • When events happen at the same time, verbs should have the same tense. Shifting incorrectly between past, present, and future tenses can confuse the reader.

Read the two descriptions of Katharen and her grandfather from Catching a Storyfish. What do you notice about the verb tenses?

Incorrect Tenses

Correct Tenses

Yesterday, Katharen and her grandfather sat and fished by the water. Her grandfather tells stories and Katharen will listen. Afterward, they walked home.

Yesterday, Katharen and her grandfather sat and fished by the water. Her grandfather told stories and Katharen listened. Afterward, they walked home.

The description starts in the past tense. In the incorrect version, the second sentence shifts out of past tense and into the present tense and future tense. In the correct version, all verbs are in the past tense.

Analyze a Conclusion

An essay is a series of paragraphs about a topic. The last paragraph of an essay is the conclusion. A strong conclusion:

  • • restates the focus statement in a new way. This sentence states the main idea of the essay by clearly answering the prompt.
  • • summarizes the key points.
  • • provides a final thought that shows the reader why the topic matters.

Focus Statement:

People use art, such as music and painting, to express their personal stories, share their cultural experiences, and connect with others.

Conclusion:

In conclusion, people use art to share their personal stories and experiences. In “The Roots of Rap,” hip hop artists use rap to tell about their lives and the challenges they face. Similarly, Cy Thao uses his artwork to share the history of the Hmong people and their struggles as refugees. These examples show how art helps people connect with others by telling stories that are meaningful to them. Whether through songs or paintings, art lets people share who they are and where they come from. Art helps us understand others’ experiences and cultures. Art is a powerful tool to tell our stories and see the world through other people’s eyes.
