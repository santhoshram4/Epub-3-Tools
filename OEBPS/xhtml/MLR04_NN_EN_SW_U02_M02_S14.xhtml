  • • A novel is a long story with characters, setting, and a plot.
  • • A novel in verse is a type of novel that is written as a series of poems and stanzas instead of sentences and paragraphs.

Explain Narrative Elements

Narrative elements are the main parts of a story. When readers are aware of narrative elements in a story, they can follow the story more easily and get more enjoyment out of it.

  • • A character is a person, animal, or creature in a story.
  • • The setting is where and when a story takes place.
  • • A conflict is a challenge or problem that a character faces.
  • • The plot is the series of events that happen in a story.
  • • A theme is an important message or moral about life that readers learn from a story.
